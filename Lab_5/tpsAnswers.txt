1. What do the `.data`, `.word`, and `.text` directives mean?**

- `.data` marks the section where variables and data are stored in memory.
- `.word` reserves space for a 32-bit (4-byte) integer and can initialize it with a value.
- `.text` marks the section containing the program's instructions.

8. Line 19 and 21 use the `syscall` instruction. What is it and how do you use it?**

`syscall` requests a service from the MARS simulator, such as printing an integer or ending the program. You place the service number in register `$v0`, put any required arguments in registers such as `$a0`, and execute `syscall`. For example, service `1` prints an integer stored in `$a0`, while service `10` exits the program.
